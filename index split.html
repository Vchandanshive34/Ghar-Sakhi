Before you join

Questions, answered honestly.

When will GharSakhi launch?

We're in the founding phase: lining up verified helpers and building this waitlist before writing a single line of booking software. Joining now tells us which sectors to prioritise first.

How are helpers verified?

Aadhaar verification and a background check, plus training on GharSakhi's service checklist — all before a helper is listed, not after a complaint.

Will my society's security be able to verify the helper?

That's the point. Every booking is designed to arrive with a name, photo and verified ID your guard can check the same way they'd check in any pre-approved visitor.

Do I need a contract?

No. The plan is hourly booking with no subscriptions and no minimum commitment.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

No security deposit is planned. You pay for the hours you book, nothing upfront.

How does hourly booking work?

You choose a service and a time slot, and pay only for the hours you use — no monthly packages, no negotiating a flat rate.

Which areas of Ulwe will you cover?

We're launching in Ulwe only, sector by sector, prioritising wherever the waitlist fills up fastest. Tell us your sector when you join.

What happens if my helper cancels?

Backup is the whole reason a platform beats one person's phone number — part of what we're building is a bench of verified helpers per sector so a cancellation doesn't mean an empty week. That matching logic is still being built; for now, tell us if reliability is your biggest concern so we weight it accordingly.

Can I request the same helper again?

That's the plan — rebooking a helper you've already had should be the easiest option, not a special request. We'll confirm this the moment it's live.
