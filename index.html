02 · The gap

A fast-growing node, an old-fashioned way to find help

01

No verified option nearby

Search for a maid service from an Ulwe address and you'll mostly find apps that don't cover this node yet, or a Facebook group asking "does anyone know a reliable bai?" — no ID checks, no ratings, no recourse if it goes wrong.

02

Gated societies need paperwork

Every new tower here runs its own visitor and staff verification at the security desk. Without an ID badge or a booking record to show the guard, a first-time helper can take longer to get past the gate than the clean itself.

03

No time to manage it

Ulwe households are commuting to BKC, the airport corridor, or across the harbour. There's no time left to interview candidates, negotiate a monthly rate, or scramble for backup when someone doesn't show up.

10 · Questions

Before you join

When does GharSakhi actually launch in Ulwe?

We're in the founding phase: lining up verified helpers and building this waitlist before writing a single line of booking software. Joining now tells us which sectors to prioritise first.

Where do the helpers come from?

We're working with established local agencies and independent helpers already active around Ulwe — not hiring cold off the street. Every one of them is verified before their first booking.

Will this work with my society's security desk?

That's the point. Every booking is designed to arrive with a name, photo and verified ID your guard can check the same way they'd check in any pre-approved visitor.

Do I have to sign a contract or pay a deposit?

No. The plan is hourly booking with no subscriptions, no security deposits and no minimum commitment.

Why only Ulwe, and not Kharghar or Panvel too?

Because a domestic-help network lives or dies on how well its helpers are vetted and matched locally. We'd rather do one node properly than stretch thin across Navi Mumbai on day one.

What happens right after I join the waitlist?

You'll hear directly from the founder as sectors, pricing and launch dates get locked in — a real reply, not an automated newsletter.
